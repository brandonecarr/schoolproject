**[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("we") provides Cohort, software that helps microschools run their programs
and prepare ESA reimbursement. Effective [DATE].

## What we collect
- **From schools/staff:** name, email, and account credentials.
- **About students (entered by the school and, with consent, the student):** name, grade,
  attendance, coursework and submissions, teacher observations, and work samples.
- **From guardians:** name, email, and tuition/payment records.

## How we use it
Only to provide the service: running the school's roster and coursework, assembling ESA invoice
packets, and tracking reimbursement. **We do not sell personal information, and we do not use student
data for advertising or to build non-educational profiles.**

## Children's privacy (COPPA)
Student accounts are created only by a verified parent/guardian, establishing verifiable parental
consent before a child logs in or submits work. Parents may review their child's information, request
corrections, and request deletion at any time through the parent portal or by contacting the school.
We retain children's data only as long as reasonably necessary (see [RETENTION.md](./RETENTION.md))
and delete it on schedule or on request.

## The school's role
The school is the entity with the direct relationship to families and directs how student data is
used; we act as its service provider / school official under a Data Processing Agreement
([DPA.md](./DPA.md)).

## Sharing
We share data only with the subprocessors needed to run the service (see [DPA.md](./DPA.md) §
Subprocessors), each under contract. We do not otherwise disclose personal information except as
required by law.
